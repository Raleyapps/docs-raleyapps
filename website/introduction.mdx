RaleyApps builds 5 apps for Jira and Jira Service Management (JSM), available on the Atlassian Marketplace. Use these docs to install an app, configure it, and fix problems on your own. Every app follows the same structure: an overview, a getting started guide, task guides, reference pages, and troubleshooting.

## Find the right app

Each app solves one problem. Pick yours.
