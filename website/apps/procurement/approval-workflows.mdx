## Overview

Purchase orders can be routed through single or multi-level approval before they are actioned. Approval rules can vary by department.

## Submitting for Approval

1. Open a saved purchase order and confirm the order lines are correct
2. Click **Submit for Approval** — the status changes to **Pending Approval**
3. The assigned approver is notified
4. The approver opens the order and clicks **Approve** or **Reject**
5. On approval, the status advances to **Approved**

## Multi-Level Approval (Approval Tiers)

For approvals that require more than one approver, configure **Approval Tiers**:

1. Go to **Project Settings → Purchase Orders → Approval Tiers**
2. Define the approval stages and the role or approver for each

Approval rules can be matched to a purchase order's **department**, so different departments can follow different approval paths.

## Troubleshooting

If an order is stuck in **Pending Approval**, confirm the required approver role is assigned and that the approval configuration matches the order's department. See [Troubleshooting](/apps/procurement/troubleshooting).
