## Overview

When ordered items arrive, record their receipt against the approved purchase order. Partial deliveries are supported.

## Recording Receipt

1. Open the approved purchase order
2. Click **Receive Goods**
3. Enter the quantities received for each order line — partial receipts are supported
4. Click **Save**

The received quantities are recorded and the purchase order status updates accordingly.
