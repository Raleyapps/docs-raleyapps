## Overview

Raley Procurement for Jira & JSM uses app-level roles to control who can configure, create, approve, and view purchase orders within a Jira project. Roles are assigned per project.

## Roles

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Finance** | Configure the app (currency, taxes, suppliers, products) and create and manage purchase orders. Required for most setup and creation tasks. |
| **Approval** | Review and approve or reject purchase orders that have been submitted for approval. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to purchase orders and related data. |

## Assigning Roles

1. Open **Project Settings** in your Jira project
2. Go to **Purchase Orders → User Roles**
3. Assign each user or group to the appropriate role

## Important Notes

- If no app-level roles are configured, all project members default to full access. Assign explicit roles to restrict access.
- Users with the **Jira Project Administrator** role bypass app-level restrictions by design. Limit this role in **Project Settings → People** where needed.
