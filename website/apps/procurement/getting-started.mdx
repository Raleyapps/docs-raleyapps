## Overview

This guide walks you through the initial setup of Raley Procurement for Jira & JSM, from installation to creating your first purchase order. Each step links to the detailed page for that area.

## Step 1 — Install the App

Install **Raley Procurement for Jira & JSM** from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com) into your Jira instance.

## Step 2 — Set Your Default Currency

Before creating any orders, set the project's Default Currency — it cannot be changed once orders exist. See [Currency Management](/apps/procurement/currency-management).

## Step 3 — Configure Tax Rates

Define your tax rates (VAT, sales tax, etc.) and mark one as the default. See [Tax Management](/apps/procurement/tax-management).

## Step 4 — Add Suppliers and Products

Build your supplier and product catalog so orders can be created from curated data. See [Managing Suppliers and Products](/apps/procurement/managing-suppliers-and-products), or [import products in bulk via CSV](/apps/procurement/importing-products-from-csv).

## Step 5 — Assign User Roles

Decide who can create, approve, and manage purchase orders. See [User Roles and Permissions](/apps/procurement/user-roles).

## Step 6 — Create Your First Purchase Order

With setup complete, create and submit your first PO. See [Create and manage purchase orders](/apps/procurement/purchase-orders).
