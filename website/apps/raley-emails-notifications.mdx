## Overview

Raley Emails Notifications lets you send fully customized emails and Slack messages straight from your Jira or Jira Service Management (JSM) projects — without leaving the tool your team already uses.

## What You Can Do

- Send **email attachments** directly from customer requests
- Distribute **Issue PDF snapshots** in notifications
- Notify users automatically on **workflow transitions**
- Set up **scheduled digest notifications**
- Include full **comment history** in outgoing emails

## Key Features

### Email Templates
Build rich templates using standard Jira fields, custom fields, and asset fields. Add your company logo, colors, and styling to keep emails on-brand.

### Recipients & Routing
- Add external email addresses and distribution lists
- Set dynamic **From** name and address
- Include **CC** and **BCC** recipients

### Workflow Integration
Trigger emails automatically when issues move through workflow steps. Include approval and decline links for JSM service requests.

### Advanced Options
- Group related activity into a **single digest email** instead of multiple notifications
- Integrate with your own **SMTP server**
- Connect with **Gmail OAuth** for email threading via Message-ID tracking

## Getting Started

1. Install Raley Emails Notifications from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com)
2. Open the app configuration in your Jira project settings
3. Create your first email template
4. Set up a workflow trigger or schedule
