## Overview

Raley Favorites lets Jira users create and manage a personal list of their most important issues. Instead of searching or scrolling through large backlogs, you can pin the issues that matter most and access them instantly.

## How to Use

### Step 1 — Install the App
Get **Favourites for Jira** from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com) and install it in your Jira instance.

### Step 2 — Add Issues to Your Favorites
1. Open any Jira issue you want to save
2. Click **More** in the issue menu
3. Select **Toggle Favourite**

The issue is now added to your personal favorites list.

### Step 3 — Access Your Favorites
1. Go to your Jira project view
2. Click **Apps** in the top navigation
3. Select **Favorites**

Your saved issues appear in one place, ready to open with a single click.

## Who Is This For?

Raley Favorites is ideal for:
- Team leads who track the same set of issues daily
- Developers juggling multiple active tickets
- Anyone who wants to reduce time spent navigating Jira
