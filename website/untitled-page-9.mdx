Short answers, with links to the page that goes deeper. Question not here? Ask us via the [support page](/support) and we will answer and add it.

## Can my teammates see my favourites list?

The list is your own. Starring a work item changes nothing on the work item and nothing in anyone else's view of Jira. The app stores your starred work item IDs in Atlassian's app storage, not on the work item.

## Where is my list stored, and does any data leave Atlassian?

In Atlassian's app storage service, as numeric work item IDs only. Nothing leaves the Atlassian platform. The [reference page](/apps/raley-favorites/reference) has the full data storage table, and the [application scopes page](/policies/Application-scopes#raley-favourites-for-jira) lists each permission.

## Does starring a work item notify anyone or make me a watcher?

No. Starring does not modify the work item, so it cannot add you as a watcher, and nothing in the published feature set sends notifications. It only puts the work item on your list.

## Do I need to configure anything after installing?

No. No settings screen or admin setup is documented; install, star, done. The [getting started page](/apps/raley-favorites/getting-started) walks the first 5 minutes.

## How many work items can I star?

No limit is documented. If you hit one in practice, tell us via the [support page](/support).

## Can I export or share my list?

The published feature set has no export or sharing. Your list lives in the **Favourites** view in the project sidebar.
