This page defines how RaleyApps classifies support requests and how quickly you can expect a first response. It applies to every request, for every customer, across all 5 apps:

- Raley Email Notifications for Jira & JSM
- Raley Intake Forms for Jira & JSM
- Raley Procurement - Intake & Approval Orchestration for JSM
- Raley Bookman - Asset Reservation for JSM
- Raley Favourites for Jira

Contact channels, business hours, and the scope of what support does and does not handle are listed on the [support page](/support). Response targets on this page are measured in business days as defined there.

## Severity levels

Every request is assigned 1 of 4 severity levels. State the impact you are seeing when you raise the request; RaleyApps confirms the severity against the definitions below and tells you if it differs from your assessment.

| Severity | Definition | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Severity 1 (Critical) | The app is unusable in production for all users and no workaround exists, data is lost or corrupted, or you suspect a security vulnerability. | The app fails to load on every screen; a suspected data exposure. |
| Severity 2 (High) | A core function is broken for many users. A workaround may exist but is not practical for daily use. | A primary feature returns errors for most users; configuration changes do not save. |
| Severity 3 (Medium) | A function is impaired for some users, or behaves incorrectly in specific conditions, and a reasonable workaround exists. | A feature fails only for 1 project or 1 configuration; incorrect display that does not block work. |
| Severity 4 (Low) | A question, a cosmetic issue, a documentation gap, or a feature request. Day-to-day use is not affected. | How-to questions; text or layout glitches; suggestions. |

Suspected security vulnerabilities are always treated as Severity 1 regardless of how many users are affected. Report them through the [support page](/support) channels and do not post details publicly.

## Response targets

| Severity | First response | Update cadence until resolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Severity 1 (Critical) | 1 business day | Every business day |
| Severity 2 (High) | 1 business day | Every 2 business days |
| Severity 3 (Medium) | 2 business days | Every 5 business days |
| Severity 4 (Low) | 3 business days | When the status changes |

These targets meet the response bar Atlassian defines in its Cloud Fortified program for critical issues: a response within 24 hours, 5 days a week.

### What the targets mean

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| First response | A team member has read your request, confirmed the severity, and started diagnosis. An automated acknowledgement does not count as a first response. |
| Business day | A working day within the business hours published on the [support page](/support). Requests raised outside business hours start the clock at the next business day. |
| Update | A substantive note on your request: what was found, what is being tried next, or what is needed from you. |
| Resolved | A fix is released, a workaround you accept is in place, or the behavior is confirmed as working as designed. |

## Resolution

Resolution times depend on the nature of the issue and are targets, not guarantees. For Severity 1 issues, work continues during business hours until a fix or an acceptable workaround is in place. Where a fix requires an app release, the update note tells you the expected release window.

## Escalation

If a request is not progressing or its impact has grown, reply on your existing request and ask for escalation, stating what changed. RaleyApps re-evaluates the severity and adjusts the response handling to match. There is no separate escalation channel; the [support page](/support) channels are the single route in.
